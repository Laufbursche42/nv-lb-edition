Speed-Freischaltung
Schaltet die XT5-Familie über 0x58 in ihren schnellen Fahrmodus (Fahrmodus 4). Der XT5 Ultra öffnet seine Höchstgeschwindigkeit per Bluetooth ohne Flashen - über die App-Drossel (Gang 4). Zum Prüfen am Roller 3x auf den Tacho tippen: läuft er dann schnell, ist er schon offen, sonst über die App öffnen. Ein Controller-Flash ist dafür nicht nötig. Bei XT5 Pro und XT5 Max ist er aus dem Code belegt, aber noch nicht am Gerät geprüft. Bei allen anderen Modellen passiert nichts.
Der Wert ist fest, es gibt nichts einzustellen: der Roller wählt ihn selbst aus seinem SKU-Byte (40, 50, 60 oder 65), der Controller rundet auf seine nächste Stufe. Der Zustand steht nur im Arbeitsspeicher, ein Druck auf die Modus-Taste oder ein Neustart setzt ihn zurück. Also vor jeder Fahrt neu senden.
Nur auf privatem Grund. Mit dieser Freischaltung fährt der Roller schneller als zugelassen. Damit erlischt die Betriebserlaubnis und der Versicherungsschutz; auf öffentlichen Wegen ist das nicht erlaubt.

Beginner's Guide

New here? You have Android, so this app does it all - connect, open your scooter and flash it, nothing leaves your phone. What is possible depends on your model.

1. Connect

Scooter on, Bluetooth on, keep it close. Settings, then Connect scooter, then pick yours. The app now knows your model.

2. Find your route

Find your model. It tells you Bluetooth-only or flash, and switchable or permanent.

XT5 Ultra
Bluetooth only, no flash. Triple-tap the speed tile: 22 to full (~50). Hardware-confirmed.
XT5 Pro or XT5 Max
Bluetooth on controller 0.0.1.0. On 0.0.1.3, flash the patch first.
NT5 Max, Max+, Ultra or Turbo
Flash the controller, then switch from the app. Re-locks to 22 on restart.
ST3, GT3, ST5, GT5 Pro/Max, UT5 Max or NT5 Ultra X
Flash the meter, then switch from the app. ST3, GT3 and GT5 can instead flash the controller (permanent).
UT5 Ultra X
Bluetooth via the gear. Still limited? Flash the controller first.
Any other (G5, V, N65i, E, S, K100, UT3, NT3 ...)
Flash the controller. Permanent, no app toggle.

Most models are experimental, not yet hardware-confirmed. Kick-start needs the meter patch. Account-bound scooters need your NAVEE User ID in Settings - not the NAVEE ID shown in the NAVEE app. Only flash a controller you can recover over SWD.

3. In the patcher

The patcher detects your model and offers, under "What to patch", only what it can take. Tick any combination.

Unlock speed
Raises the controller top speed - pick the km/h. Controller.
Switchable speed limit (Bluetooth)
Makes the top speed switchable live, no re-flash. Meter.
Kick-start
Pull away from standstill. Meter.
Cruise control
Holds your speed, where supported. Meter.
Silence beeps
Mute single tones; the confirmation beep stays. Meter.

Speed sits in the controller, the rest in the meter - the app flashes only the chips you touch, controller first, meter last. A missing option means your model does not have it. A firmware version other than the installed one is flagged as untested.

4. Download, patch, flash

Tap Download and patch: the app fetches the original firmware and patches a copy on your phone - both land in your Downloads folder. Then it hands off to the flasher: tick consent and start. Nothing is uploaded.

5. Switchable or permanent

Switchable: triple-tap the speed tile to flip between a locked speed and your open speed. On XT5, ST3/GT3, GT5, ST5 and UT5 Max you set both - your locked limit (e.g. 20, 22 or 25 for your country) and your open speed. Permanent: the higher speed stays until you flash stock back.

6. Back to stock

To undo everything, flash the manufacturer firmware back. The original .bin was saved to your Downloads folder when you patched (or just re-download it). Load it with no option ticked and flash - the result is byte-identical to factory, so the speed limit and the road approval (ABE) are back. Meter and controller can be restored on their own.

At your own risk: on many models patching is experimental and not hardware-confirmed. A higher speed voids the road approval. Private ground, your own scooter only.
